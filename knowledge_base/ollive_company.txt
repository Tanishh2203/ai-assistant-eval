=====================================================

Tagline: Don't just ship AI, insure it!

What is Ollive?
----------------
Ollive is an AI liability insurance company. Their core product is AI Liability Insurance — a dedicated insurance policy designed specifically for companies that build and deploy AI systems. 

The core problem Ollive solves: Enterprise buyers close faster when they trust your AI is covered. Traditional insurance policies (cyber, E&O) were not designed for AI-specific failures like hallucinations, algorithmic bias, copyright violations, or autonomous agent failures. Ollive provides the policy-backed proof enterprise buyers need.

The Market Problem
-------------------
AI lawsuits grew 978% from 2021 to 2025. Over 700 active GenAI cases are in US courts today.

In 2025, major insurers including AIG, Chubb, and WR Berkley introduced "absolute AI exclusions" — meaning standard cyber and E&O policies will no longer respond to AI-related claims. Courts are increasingly holding AI vendors (not just deployers) liable for their AI's outputs, including hallucinations, biased decisions, and IP violations.

None of these risks are covered by existing standard policies. Ollive was built to fill this gap.

What Ollive Covers
-------------------
Ollive's AI Liability Insurance provides dedicated coverage for the ways AI systems actually fail. The six coverage areas are:

1. Hallucination & Output Liability
Coverage for third-party claims arising from harmful, misleading, or factually incorrect AI outputs that cause financial or reputational harm. Includes legal defense, settlements, and customer claims. Example: An AI chatbot invents a policy or fact that causes a user financial harm.

2. Algorithmic Bias & Discrimination
Coverage for claims involving discriminatory outcomes from AI-driven decisions in hiring, lending, pricing, or access to services. Includes legal defense, settlements, and regulatory response. Example: An AI hiring tool rejects candidates based on age, race, or gender patterns in training data.

3. Copyright & IP Claims
Coverage for copyright, trade dress, and IP infringement claims arising from content your AI system generates for you or your customers. Includes IP defense and settlement costs. Real-world scenario: AI-generated marketing assets produced for a client closely resemble a competitor's copyrighted creative work. A lawsuit follows.

4. AI Regulatory Investigations
Coverage for AI-related regulatory proceedings, investigations, and enforcement actions under emerging AI laws and existing consumer protection frameworks. Includes defense costs, certain fines, and proceedings. Example: A regulator investigates your AI system for consumer protection violations.

5. Sensitive Data Disclosure
Coverage for unauthorized disclosure of confidential or regulated information through AI outputs, distinct from a traditional data breach event. Includes incident response, legal costs, and privacy claims. Example: An AI system surfaces private user data in its responses to other users.

6. AI Incident Response
First-party coverage for immediate operational response when an AI system misfires — forensic analysis, crisis communications, and remediation costs. Includes forensics, remediation, and crisis management.

Why Existing Insurance Falls Short
------------------------------------
Cyber insurance: Designed for data breaches and network intrusions, not AI output errors or hallucinations.
E&O (Errors & Omissions): Not designed for AI-native failures like algorithmic bias or training data copyright claims.
Employment practices liability: Not designed for algorithmic discrimination in AI hiring tools.
General liability: Does not address AI-specific regulatory proceedings or output liability.

The gap: Courts are holding AI vendors liable. Regulators are acting. And the standard insurance market is adding AI exclusions, not coverage. Ollive exists to fill that gap with purpose-built AI liability coverage.
